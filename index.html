Introduction

ICT stands for Information and Communication Technology. It covers all the tools we use to collect, store, process, share and communicate information, such as computers, smartphones, the internet, software, networks and digital media.

Today almost every part of life depends on ICT. From studying and banking to shopping and healthcare, technology has changed how people live and work. For this reason, learning ICT is no longer optional. It is a basic skill for every student.

Benefits of Learning ICT

  • Improves problem-solving, logical thinking and creativity.
  • Makes research, assignments and presentations faster and better.
  • Increases job opportunities and the chance of earning online as a freelancer.
  • Helps in using the internet safely and wisely.
  • Saves time and effort through automation and digital tools.
  • Prepares students for higher education and a technology-driven future.
